How it works

From a photo on your phone to a concept you can walk through.

No modelling. No menus with two hundred icons. Just the room, your idea, and a few minutes.

01

Bring what you have

A floor plan, a photo of the room, a Pinterest board, a napkin sketch. Forma reads walls, windows and doors automatically.

02

Say what you want

Talk to it the way you'd brief a junior: "warmer", "more seating", "keep the fireplace". Every change is editable, nothing is locked in.

03

Explore and share

Flip between layouts, swap materials, walk the 3D concept. Send a link to your client before you've spent a day in a drawing tool.

Why designers love it

Less time in software. More time designing.

Floor plan → editable 3D space

Drop in any plan and get a proportioned 3D model in seconds — walls, openings and ceiling heights included.

Try layouts instantly

Ask for three seating arrangements and compare them side by side. Move one sofa and everything else updates.

Explore styles and materials

Warm minimal, Japandi, coastal, mid-century. Swap oak for walnut, linen for bouclé, and see it in the actual room.

Show clients concepts earlier

Share a link on day one instead of week three. Fewer surprises at the reveal, faster sign-off, happier clients.

Explore styles

Same room. Five moods. One click each.

Pick a direction and Forma restyles the whole space — palette, materials, furniture language — while keeping your layout intact.
