FAQ

Questions designers ask before they try it

Do I need to know CAD or SketchUp?

No. If you can describe a room to a client, you can use Forma. You upload, you describe, you drag things around. The technical drawing happens underneath and you can export it when someone needs it.

Will it look like generic AI, or like my work?

Forma restyles your actual room from your actual references — your Pinterest board, your palette, your furniture picks. It's a fast pair of hands for your direction, not a random image generator.

Can I start from just a photo of the room?

Yes. A phone photo, a floor plan, a listing PDF, or a sketch on paper all work. Forma reads walls, windows and doors and builds an editable space you can measure.

What do I actually get out at the end?

Presentation-ready boards and renders (PNG, PDF), a 3D link your client can walk through on their phone, and on Studio, editable SketchUp/DWG files for your drafter or contractor.

Who owns the designs?

You do. Everything you generate is yours to present, sell and reuse. We never train on your client projects.

Are client photos and plans private?

Projects are private by default and only visible through links you create. You can delete a project and its files at any time.
